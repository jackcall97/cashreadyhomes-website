## Understanding the Cash Offer Formula

To know if a cash offer is fair, you first need to understand how professional cash buying companies calculate their numbers. Reputable investors do not pull numbers out of thin air; they use a standard industry formula known as the **After Repair Value (ARV) formula**.

At its core, the formula looks like this:

`Fair Cash Offer = (After Repair Value) - (Repair Costs) - (Holding & Selling Costs) - (Minimum Profit Margin)`

Let’s break down each component of this equation so you can see how it applies to your Westchester County home:

### 1. After Repair Value (ARV)
The ARV is what your property is realistically expected to sell for on the open market *after* it has been fully renovated and updated. Cash buyers look at recent comparable sales ("comps") of fully remodeled homes in your immediate neighborhood—whether you are in Yonkers, White Plains, or New Rochelle—to establish this baseline figure.

### 2. Estimated Repair Costs
This is the cost to fix up the property, address deferred maintenance, update outdated kitchens or bathrooms, and repair structural or cosmetic flaws. Because cash buyers typically purchase homes "as-is," they take on the burden, risk, and expense of hiring contractors and managing renovations. These estimated repair costs are subtracted from the ARV.

### 3. Holding and Selling Costs
Even when a home is purchased with cash, investors face various expenses when they eventually resell it. These include closing costs, property taxes, utility bills, insurance, loan servicing fees, and real estate agent commissions when they list the property later. Generally, these costs account for roughly 5% to 10% of the home's final value.

### 4. Profit Margin
As a business, cash buying companies must factor in a reasonable profit margin to cover their operational overhead, team salaries, and market risks. Real estate markets fluctuate, and taking on a distressed property carries inherent financial risk.

## Key Factors That Influence Your Westchester County Cash Offer

No two homes are alike, and several localized factors will dictate the exact cash offer you receive for your property:

* **Location and Neighborhood Demand:** Westchester County features hyper-local micro-markets. Proximity to Metro-North train stations, top-tier school districts, and major highways heavily impacts property value, even in an as-is condition.
* **Structural and Cosmetic Condition:** A home needing a complete roof replacement, new plumbing, or mold remediation will receive a lower cash offer than a structurally sound home that merely needs cosmetic updates.
* **Scope of Renovations Needed:** The more labor and materials required to bring the property up to modern market standards, the higher the deduction from the ARV.
* **Current Market Trends:** Real estate inventory levels and local buyer demand across the Hudson Valley dictate how quickly an investor can turn around and resell the property.
