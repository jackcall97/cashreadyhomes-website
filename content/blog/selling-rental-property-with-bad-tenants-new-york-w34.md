To make matters worse, New York State has some of the strictest tenant protection laws in the country, making swift evictions difficult and time-consuming.

## The Realities of New York Landlord-Tenant Laws

Before making a move, it is vital to understand the playing field. New York's legal framework heavily favors tenant rights, particularly under regulations like the Housing Stability and Tenant Protection Act (HSTPA). 

* **Strict Notice Requirements:** If you want to non-renew a month-to-month tenant or terminate a tenancy, New York law mandates strict written notice timelines based on how long the tenant has lived there (ranging from 30 to 90 days).
* **The Eviction Bottleneck:** Filing a holdover or non-payment proceeding in local housing courts can take months—sometimes longer if court backlogs occur. 
* **Prohibition of "Self-Help":** Changing the locks, shutting off utilities, or forcibly removing a tenant's belongings is completely illegal in New York and can result in severe penalties and lawsuits.

Because of these hurdles, listing a tenant-occupied property on the open market with a traditional real estate agent often leads to failed deals, stressed-out buyers, and prolonged holding costs.

## Your Options for Selling a Tenant-Occupied Property

When you are ready to cut ties with a problematic rental, you generally have a few different paths to consider:

### 1. Wait Out the Lease
If your bad tenant is still bound by a fixed-term lease, you can technically wait until the lease expiration date to sell. However, if they refuse to vacate once the term ends, you will still face a holdover situation. For landlords dealing with active property damage or zero rent collection, waiting months is often not financially viable.

### 2. Offer a Cash-for-Keys Agreement
Sometimes, offering a financial incentive can persuade a difficult tenant to pack up and leave voluntarily. A "cash-for-keys" agreement involves paying the tenant a designated sum of cash once they hand over the keys and vacate the property in broom-clean condition. While this can fast-track a vacancy, it relies entirely on the tenant's cooperation and costs you money upfront.

### 3. Sell As-Is to a Specialized Cash Buyer
The fastest, least stressful way to deal with this headache is to sell the property directly to a professional cash home-buying company. You do not need to evict the tenants, clean up the property, or coordinate messy open houses.
